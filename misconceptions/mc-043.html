MISCONCEPTION MC-043 — Institutions
MYTH

Tokenized assets can function on a single chain without cross-chain infrastructure.

THE REALITY

DTCC's Smart NAV pilot explicitly required chain-agnostic delivery as a design requirement. The goal was to disseminate NAV data across virtually any private or public blockchain simultaneously. DTCC stated the core capability is applicable across endless use cases and could power a broader range of use cases beyond NAV data across a greater number of blockchains. Without interoperability, tokenized data stays siloed on individual chains.
