Chainlink C.R.E. Explained

The missing OS for Web3 — connecting DeFi to TradFi in 1 minute.

DESCRIPTION

The Chainlink Runtime Environment (CRE) is the orchestration layer that connects enterprise systems, blockchains, and Chainlink services into unified institutional workflows. Think of it as the operating system layer between TradFi and DeFi — the piece that makes Chainlink capable of running DTCC's Collateral AppChain, the Reserve Bank of Australia's Project Acacia, and complex cross-chain settlement workflows. This Short explains what CRE is, why institutions need it, and why it represents Chainlink's expansion beyond oracle data delivery into full workflow orchestration.

TRANSCRIPT

DeFi has blockchains. TradFi has legacy systems. What connects them? The Chainlink Runtime Environment — CRE — is the missing operating system for institutional blockchain. It orchestrates workflows across any chain, any system, any institution. DTCC uses it. The Reserve Bank of Australia uses it. It turns Chainlink from a data provider into infrastructure. That's CRE.
