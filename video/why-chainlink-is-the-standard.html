CCIP

Why Chainlink Is The Standard

One messaging and settlement standard across Web3, finance, and AI.

DESCRIPTION

CCIP — the Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol — is emerging as the standard for cross-chain messaging and token transfer because it is the only cross-chain protocol with an independently-operated Risk Management Network (RMN) that re-verifies every message before it executes. Coinbase uses CCIP to wrap $7B+ in assets across chains. Circle's CCTP for native USDC transfers is built on CCIP. DTCC's Smart NAV pilot used CCIP across 10 major financial institutions. Swift has explored CCIP for cross-border settlement. This Short explains why the combination of security, adoption, and institutional trust makes CCIP the de facto standard for cross-chain communication.

TRANSCRIPT

Every major chain. Every major institution. One standard. CCIP. Coinbase uses it to wrap $7 billion in assets. Swift tested it for cross-border settlement. DTCC ran the Smart NAV pilot on it with 10 banks. Circle's native USDC transfer runs through it. CCIP has an independent Risk Management Network — a second layer of verification no other bridge has. One messaging standard. For Web3, finance, and AI. That standard is Chainlink.
