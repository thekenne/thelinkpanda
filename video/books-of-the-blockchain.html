The Books of the Blockchain

Every blockchain is a book. Chainlink is the librarian connecting every shelf.

DESCRIPTION

Blockchains are like books — each one stores a complete record, but they cannot read each other. Ethereum doesn't know what Solana contains. Bitcoin doesn't know what Avalanche processed. The result is a fragmented digital infrastructure where value and data are siloed by design. Chainlink acts as the librarian — the universal translation and delivery layer that allows any blockchain to read, verify, and act on information from any other blockchain or off-chain system. This Short uses the library metaphor to explain why a neutral infrastructure layer is necessary and why Chainlink occupies that role.

TRANSCRIPT

Imagine every blockchain as a different book. Each one complete. Each one unreadable by the others. Ethereum can't read Solana. Bitcoin can't read Avalanche. Data stays trapped on whichever chain it was written on. Chainlink is the librarian. It reads every book. Translates across every system. Delivers verified truth from any source to any chain. Every book. Every shelf. One librarian.
