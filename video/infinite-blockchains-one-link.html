Infinite Blockchains, One Link

Every chain is a separate island. Only Chainlink connects them all.

DESCRIPTION

Every blockchain is a separate island — Ethereum cannot talk to Avalanche, Solana cannot talk to Polygon, and traditional financial systems cannot talk to any of them. Chainlink's Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP) and the Chainlink Runtime Environment (CRE) solve this by providing a universal messaging and settlement layer that connects every major blockchain, private ledger, and enterprise system. This Short explains why interoperability is the foundational problem of the multi-chain era and how Chainlink functions as the connective tissue of Web3.
