This Is Why Chainlink Will Keep Growing

Protocols pay in LINK. Stakers lock LINK. The real-time flywheel spins faster every day.

DESCRIPTION

The Chainlink growth flywheel is driven by real demand, not speculation. As more protocols and institutions integrate Chainlink services, fee revenue increases. Fee revenue flows to node operators and into the Chainlink Reserve (accumulating LINK weekly). Stakers lock LINK to earn yield, reducing circulating supply. The BUILD programme creates additional demand from protocols committing LINK for service access. This Short explains why organic growth in Chainlink usage creates sustainable upward pressure on the network's economics rather than depending on token price speculation.

TRANSCRIPT

Every protocol that integrates Chainlink pays fees. Those fees fund node operators and build the reserve. The reserve accumulates LINK every week — 148,000 LINK last week alone. Stakers lock LINK for yield. Less supply. More demand. The BUILD programme adds protocols committing LINK for access. Real usage. Real fees. Real accumulation. The Chainlink flywheel doesn't stop. It spins faster every time a new institution goes live.
