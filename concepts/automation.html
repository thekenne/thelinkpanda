What is Chainlink Automation?
Chainlink Automation

Decentralized, condition-based smart contract execution.

This page explains what Chainlink Automation is, why it matters, who uses it in production, and what institutional evidence supports its adoption.

WHAT IS IT

Chainlink Automation (formerly Keepers) enables smart contracts to be automatically executed when predefined conditions are met — without requiring a centralized server or manual trigger. Node operators monitor conditions off-chain and submit transactions on-chain when triggers fire.

WHY IT MATTERS

Smart contracts cannot execute themselves. A liquidation that should fire when collateral falls below a threshold, a rebalancing that should trigger at month end, a payment that should execute on a schedule — all require an external trigger. Automation provides that trigger in a decentralized, trust-minimized way.

WHO USES IT

  • Compound — interest accrual and liquidation
  • ADI Predictstreet — automated market settlement for FIFA World Cup 2026
