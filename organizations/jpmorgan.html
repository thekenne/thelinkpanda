JPMorgan and Chainlink

JPMorgan Chase & Co. / Kinexys

JPMorgan is the world's largest bank by market capitalisation. Its Kinexys platform (formerly Onyx) is an institutional blockchain for tokenized deposits, repo, and cross-chain settlement.

HOW JPMORGAN USES CHAINLINK

JPMorgan Kinexys selected Chainlink CCIP for institutional cross-chain DvP (Delivery versus Payment) settlement — one of the most significant CCIP deployments by a single institution. JPMorgan also participated in the DTCC Smart NAV Pilot using Chainlink CCIP for fund data delivery.
